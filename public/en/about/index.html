Oleslav Antamoshkin

Oleslav Antamoshkin is a Software & AI Architect, Doctor of Engineering Sciences and Head of the Software Engineering Department at Siberian Federal University. His work focuses on software architecture, artificial intelligence, distributed and heterogeneous systems, agentic software engineering, computer vision, UAV-based spatial monitoring and applied AI systems.

Professional profile

He designs and leads the development of complex software and AI systems from architecture and technical specifications to implementation, integration, and deployment. His work combines engineering leadership with applied research where the resulting software, data pipelines, and experiments can be used and evaluated in practice.

Current roles

  • Head of the Software Engineering Department, Siberian Federal University.
  • Professor at the Department of Information Technologies in Creative and Cultural Industries, Siberian Federal University.
  • Scientific and technical lead for AirScope and project lead for the Siberiana digital platform.

Research interests

Software architecture, artificial intelligence, distributed and heterogeneous computing, multi-agent systems, computer vision, UAV systems, spatial monitoring, GIS and digital platforms.
