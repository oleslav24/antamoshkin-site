UAV Spatial Monitoring

UAV spatial monitoring uses aerial data, photogrammetry, 3D reconstruction, point clouds, and computer vision to assess the condition and change of real-world objects. This is the central applied domain of AirScope, led scientifically and technically by Oleslav Antamoshkin.

Relation to the work

Relevant work includes AirScope, its AirBIM construction module, optimal flight-mission planning, and UAV-based tree-species detection.
